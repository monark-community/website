Monark's expertise comes from a collaborative project between the University of Sherbrooke, EOS Nation and Vincent Grenier himself. Together they designed <a href="https://www.usherbrooke.ca/actualites/nouvelles/sciences-innovations-technologiques/details/42749" target="_blank">CryptoSys</a>, a decentralized accounting system, and the project showed how well Monark can bring academia and industry together.

> "Our collaboration with the University of Sherbrooke demonstrated the immense potential that lies in university-business partnerships," explains Vincent Grenier, founder and CEO of Monark. "We observed how students, when guided by industry professionals, can develop innovative solutions that address real market needs."

## How Monark supports student projects

Monark tailors its support to each university project. Beyond its role as a financial partner, the company acts as a technical and strategic mentor and guides students through every step of development, so the results hold up in real-world use.

> "Our method combines academic rigor with market requirements," continues Grenier. "We help students transform their theoretical knowledge into viable products, while offering them invaluable professional experience."

## New projects under Monark's wing

After these first collaborations, Monark is taking over several promising university projects in decentralized technology. These initiatives were scattered until now and will get structured guidance and technical expertise from Monark's team. They include decentralized accounting systems for complex organizations, supply chain traceability, and transparent governance applications for institutions.

## An ecosystem around the students

Monark's network of industrial and academic partners lets it build an innovation ecosystem around decentralized technologies. Students get as much contact with industry as possible while keeping the academic framework their training needs.

> "We aim to become the bridge connecting university talent to market opportunities," says Grenier. "Our vision is to foster the next generation of decentralized technology innovators by providing them with a springboard to a successful career."

## What students take away

Students who join these projects develop professional skills that employers look for, along with a network that makes it easier to start their careers in the industry.

> "Students who participate in our projects gain experience that goes well beyond what a traditional curriculum can offer," emphasizes Grenier. "They learn to manage complex projects, collaborate with different stakeholders, and solve concrete problems."

Building on past collaborations such as the one with the University of Sherbrooke, Vincent Grenier's company wants to lead the next wave of innovation in decentralized accounting and set new standards for supporting university projects in decentralized technologies.

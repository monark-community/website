## Why TrustList

Scammers generate addresses that start and end like someone you pay, then send you a tiny "dust" transfer so the fake shows up in your history. Most people compare only the first and last few characters before they copy an address. A contact list that knows the full address catches the swap before any funds move.

TrustList also keeps judgement and evidence apart. It never computes a score for a person. Your trust level is your own call, and it sits next to facts anyone can check: the owner's signed confirmation of the name you gave their address, and public vouches from people in your circles.

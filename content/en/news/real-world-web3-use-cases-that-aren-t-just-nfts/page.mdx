## Community currencies

A community currency brings blockchain's transparency and security to a local economy. Unlike traditional money, it can be programmed to reward what the community cares about, such as buying from local businesses or volunteering. That builds trust and engagement where ordinary money often falls short.

## Supply chain tracking

Consumers want to know more and more about where their products come from. [ChainProof](https://chainproof.monark.io) records each step of a supply chain on a blockchain, where the records can't be altered and can be traced. Anyone can then check authenticity, ethical sourcing and environmental impact, which holds companies accountable and gives consumers a reason to trust them.

## Notarized documents and public registries

Legal and official documents usually depend on central authorities, which makes them costly, opaque and vulnerable. [WillChain](https://willchain.monark.io) and [Cadastrum](https://cadastrum.monark.io) use a blockchain to notarize wills, land titles and other records. The result is public, tamper-proof evidence of ownership or intent, with less fraud, lower costs and easier access.

## Cooperative governance

Cooperatives run on member trust and shared governance. [CoopDao](https://coopdao.monark.io) puts that governance on a blockchain, so decisions are transparent and auditable and benefits are shared fairly. It can help restore confidence in cooperatives as democratic, sustainable businesses.

## Medical data exchange

Medical data is extremely sensitive and valuable, yet it sits in silos that make it hard to access and organize for research. [Cura](https://cura.monark.io) is a decentralized protocol that lets people securely share, sell or donate anonymized medical data using zero-knowledge proofs (a way to prove something is true without revealing the data behind it). Privacy is respected, and research, including medical AI, gets access to data it couldn't reach before.

## Verifiable credentials in education

Paper certificates and transcripts are easy to forge or lose. A verifiable credential recorded on a blockchain is a permanent record of what someone achieved, and they can carry it with them and share it. Learners, schools and employers get trusted proof without an intermediary.

## What these projects have in common

Each example solves a real problem with transparency, security and more control for users. Unlike speculative NFT projects, they show how Web3 can build trust and accountability into systems people rely on.
